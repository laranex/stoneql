# Code of Conduct

StoneQL follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews or any other project space, agrees to it.

## Our standards

We want StoneQL to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. In short:

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Do not harass, insult, demean or threaten anyone, and do not post sexualised content, personal attacks or other people's private information.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 linked above applies, including its enforcement guidelines.

## Reporting

Reports are handled by the project maintainers on GitHub; no email address is published.

- Use GitHub's **Report content** option (the **…** menu on a comment, issue or pull request) to report it to GitHub and the maintainers.
- If you need to speak to the maintainers privately, open an issue titled **"Conduct report: please contact me"** without any details, and a maintainer will arrange a private channel.

Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, keep the reporter's identity private and act according to the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
